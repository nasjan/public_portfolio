Aikani

A companion concept for seniors that helps with daily planning, voice guided support, and finding local activities.

Overview

Aikani is a digital service concept designed for independent seniors in Finland. It combines a high-contrast calendar, a voice assistant, and a local event feed. The goal is to support daily routines and reduce isolation by making technology feel safe and accessible.

Video Presentation

Concept ad for Aikani. Click to play.

Problem and Context

Many seniors in Finland live alone and find it hard to plan their days and stay socially active. Most digital tools are too complex or not designed for older users. The challenge was to create a solution that builds confidence rather than confusion.

This was a group project for the Digital Product course at Haaga-Helia UAS. We completed the concept from research to pitch in 6 weeks.

Research and Insights

The team did basic research, created six personas with empathy maps, and used the "Jobs To Be Done" method to understand user needs.

  • Visibility: Seniors need high contrast and large text.
  • Simplicity: Voice help is useful but it must be simple and predictable
  • Motivation: Social connection is a key motivator, not just task management

Key insight: The biggest problem is not technology. It is confidence. The interface must make users feel safe and sure they cannot break anything.

Ideation and Decisions

The team explored several directions and chose three main features:

  • Calendar with high contrast and large touch targets
  • Voice assistant with clear and predictable answers
  • Local events feed to encourage social activity

We co-designed the wireframes and user flows in Miro to ensure the features worked seamlessly together.

Design Solution

The final concept package included a strategic analysis, personas, user flows, and a visual identity.

  • Visual Identity Logo and visual identity for the Aikani brand
  • Promo Video A 60-second story created with AI tools (Sora for video, Suno for music, Speechify for voice) to demonstrate the user experience.
  • Pitch Deck A presentation outlining the business case and user benefits.

Results and Learnings

This project taught me how to articulate a product vision and present it effectively.

  • Process I learned how to move from a vague problem to a concrete concept.
  • AI Tools I gained practical experience in using generative AI for rapid prototyping and storytelling.
  • Collaboration: Working in a 5-person team improved my ability to give and receive design feedback.

Note: This was a group project. Everyone contributed to planning and execution.
