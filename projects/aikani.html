Aikani

A companion app for seniors that helps with daily planning, voice-assisted guidance, and finding local activities.

Overview

Aikani is designed for independent seniors in Finland who want to stay organized and connected. It brings together a simple calendar with high contrast, a voice assistant that actually helps, and a way to discover community events nearby. The goal is to support daily routines and reduce feelings of isolation.

Problem & Context

Many seniors in Finland live independently but struggle with daily planning and social isolation. Existing digital tools are often too complex or not designed with older users in mind. The challenge was to create something genuinely useful that respects the capabilities and limitations of the target audience.

This was a group project for the DigiTuote (Digital Product) course at Haaga-Helia University of Applied Sciences, completed over approximately 6 weeks in 2025.

Research & Insights

The team conducted lightweight research, created six personas with empathy maps, and used the Jobs To Be Done framework to understand user needs.

  • Seniors need high-contrast, large-text interfaces with minimal navigation depth
  • Voice assistance is valued but must be simple and predictable
  • Community connection is a key motivator — not just task management

Key insight: The biggest barrier isn't technology literacy — it's confidence. The interface must reassure users that they can't break anything.

Ideation & Decisions

The team brainstormed multiple directions and converged on three core features: a simplified calendar, a voice assistant, and a community events discovery feature. Wireframes and user flows were created collaboratively in Figma.

  • Calendar with high contrast and large touch targets
  • Voice assistant with clear, predictable responses
  • Local events feed to encourage social participation

Design Solution

The final deliverables included a concept summary, personas and JTBD analysis, user flows, wireframes, selected hi-fi screens, logo and visual identity, a 60-second promo video, and a pitch deck.

  • Selected hi-fi screens designed in Figma
  • Logo and visual identity for the Aikani brand
  • 60-second promo video created with Sora (video), Suno (music), and Speechify (voice)
  • Pitch deck summarizing the concept and rationale

Results & Learnings

The project demonstrated how to take a digital product from concept to presentation as a team. Quick peer feedback sessions helped refine the direction throughout.

  • Learned the full process of creating a digital product concept
  • Practiced AI-assisted video production with Sora, Suno, and Speechify
  • Strengthened collaboration skills in a 5-person team
  • Would add user testing with actual seniors in a future iteration

Note: This was a group project. Everyone pitched in on planning and execution.
