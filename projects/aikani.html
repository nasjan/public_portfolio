Aikani

A time management concept exploring how personal scheduling and prioritization could be reimagined through a cleaner, calmer interface.

Role
Concept, UX & Video
Timeline
Course project
Tools
Figma, Premiere Pro
Team

Problem

Time management tools feel cluttered and stressful. Users need a calmer way to organize their day.

Goal

Explore a concept for a minimal, focused scheduling interface with clear visual prioritization.

My Role

Concept development, UI design, and promotional video production.

Outcome

Concept prototype and promotional video demonstrating the core value proposition.

Overview

Aikani is a concept project exploring how time management could be simplified through a calm, minimal interface. The project included UI concept design and a short promotional video to communicate the core idea.

Problem & Context

Existing calendar and scheduling tools tend to pack screens with information, creating visual overload. The concept explores whether a stripped-back interface could reduce daily planning stress and help users focus on what matters most.

Research & Insights

I reviewed popular scheduling tools and identified common UX patterns that contribute to cognitive overload. Informal conversations with peers confirmed that many people find existing tools stressful rather than helpful.

  • Visual clutter is the primary source of scheduling anxiety
  • Users value seeing only today's priorities at a glance
  • Color-coded urgency helps quick scanning

Ideation & Decisions

The concept focuses on a single-day view with clear priority levels. I explored multiple layout options in Figma before settling on a card-based approach with gentle color coding.

  • Single-day focus reduces overwhelm
  • Card-based tasks with priority indicators
  • Minimal navigation for daily use

Design Solution

The final concept uses a clean single-column layout, soft color accents for priority levels, and large touch targets for mobile use. A promotional video was produced to present the concept and value proposition.

  • Today-focused dashboard with task cards
  • Gentle color accents for priority (not alarm-style red)
  • Promotional video created with Premiere Pro

Validation & Iteration

As a concept project, formal usability testing was not conducted. The video and prototype were reviewed by peers and the course instructor, who provided positive feedback on clarity and visual direction.

Results & Learnings

The project strengthened my ability to communicate a design concept through both static prototypes and video storytelling. Key takeaway: sometimes the best design decision is removing features, not adding them.

  • Practiced concept communication through video
  • Reinforced the value of constraint-driven design
  • Would add user testing in a future iteration
