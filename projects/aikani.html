Aikani

A companion app for seniors that helps with daily planning, voice guided support, and finding local activities.

Overview

Aikani is made for independent seniors in Finland who want to stay organized and connected. It combines a simple high contrast calendar, a voice assistant, and a way to find community events nearby. The goal is to support daily routines and help seniors feel less isolated.

Video Presentation

Concept ad for Aikani. Click to play.

Problem and Context

Many seniors in Finland live alone and find it hard to plan their days and stay socially active. Most digital tools are too complex or not designed for older users. The challenge was to make something truly useful that works well for this audience.

This was a group project for the Digital Product course at Haaga Helia University of Applied Sciences. We completed it in about 6 weeks in 2025.

Research and Insights

The team did basic research, created six personas with empathy maps, and used the Jobs To Be Done method to understand user needs.

  • Seniors need high contrast and large text with simple navigation
  • Voice help is useful but it must be simple and predictable
  • Social connection is a key motivator, not just task management

Key insight: The biggest problem is not technology. It is confidence. The interface must make users feel safe and sure they cannot break anything.

Ideation and Decisions

The team explored several directions and chose three main features: a simplified calendar, a voice assistant, and a local events feed. We made wireframes and user flows together in Figma.

  • Calendar with high contrast and large touch targets
  • Voice assistant with clear and predictable answers
  • Local events feed to encourage social activity

Design Solution

The final deliverables included a concept summary, personas and JTBD analysis, user flows, wireframes, high fidelity screens, a logo, visual identity, a 60 second promo video, and a pitch deck.

  • High fidelity screens designed in Figma
  • Logo and visual identity for the Aikani brand
  • 60 second promo video made with Sora for video, Suno for music, and Speechify for voice
  • Pitch deck with the concept and reasoning

Results and Learnings

This project showed how to take a digital product from idea to presentation as a team. Regular peer feedback helped us improve our direction throughout the project.

  • Learned the full process of creating a digital product concept
  • Practiced AI assisted video production with Sora, Suno, and Speechify
  • Improved teamwork skills in a 5 person group
  • In a future version, we would test with actual seniors

Note: This was a group project. Everyone contributed to planning and execution.
