Nosara

Instagram

Hard techno artist identity

The client wanted something rough and aggressive for her hard techno brand. We worked in quick feedback rounds. I looked at metal band logos for inspiration, used AI to explore different texture styles, then rebuilt the final direction from scratch in Illustrator and Photoshop.

  • Style: Harsh, textured wordmark inspired by metal aesthetics
  • Colors: Black, gray, white
  • Goal: Raw, bold, high impact look
  • Status: Used on flyers and merch. T shirts already produced. Delivered full kit with different formats and color versions.

Heppahubi.fi

Instagram

Horse care and substitute service

They needed a clean logo that combines a helping hand with a horse head. I sketched several ideas, got feedback from the client, and refined the chosen concept into a simple and friendly symbol that works at any size.

  • Style: Simple line icon with calm, balanced geometry
  • Colors: Green main version, plus black and white variant
  • Goal: Feel trustworthy and caring, stay clear even at small sizes
  • Status: Delivered and in active use. Full file kit with variants and formats.

Mentalink

Therapy matching concept (student project)

After we chose the name, I designed a logo that connects the ideas of mind and connection. The brain shape has a link symbol in the center to show what the product does.

  • Style: Clean, geometric icon
  • Colors: Blue as the main color
  • Typography: Montserrat font family
  • Goal: Look professional and trustworthy
  • Status: Used throughout the course project.

Saumaton

Fictional UX and web agency for a course site

For this fictional agency project, I created a modern mark built around the letter S. The continuous form inside a circle suggests seamless service.

  • Style: Modern monogram mark
  • Colors: Dark gray and white
  • Goal: Professional and clean look
  • Status: Used on the course WordPress site prototype.
