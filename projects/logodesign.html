Logo Design

Brand identity projects exploring logo concepts, visual systems, and design rationale — from brief to final mark.

Role
Visual Designer
Timeline
Various projects
Tools
Illustrator, Figma
Team
Solo

Problem

Each project needed a distinct visual identity that communicates brand values clearly and works across media.

Goal

Create memorable, versatile logos with clear design rationale and consistent visual language.

My Role

Concept development, sketching, digital execution, and presentation of final designs.

Outcome

Multiple completed logo projects with documentation of the design process and reasoning.

Overview

This collection showcases logo design projects completed across coursework and personal practice. Each project followed a structured process from understanding the brief through research, concept sketching, refinement, and final delivery.

Problem & Context

Each logo project started with a different brief and set of constraints. The common challenge was translating abstract brand values into a single, recognizable visual mark that works at all sizes and across different applications.

Research & Insights

For each project I reviewed the competitive landscape, analyzed visual trends in the relevant industry, and identified qualities the brand needed to communicate.

  • Competitive visual audit for each project
  • Mood boards to align on visual direction
  • Key brand attributes translated into visual principles

Ideation & Decisions

Each project involved extensive sketching and concept exploration before moving to digital. Multiple directions were explored and narrowed based on alignment with brand values and versatility requirements.

  • Rapid sketching to explore many directions
  • Concept refinement based on brand fit
  • Scalability and versatility testing at early stages

Design Solution

Final logos were crafted in vector format with attention to grid alignment, optical balance, and consistent stroke weights. Each delivery included usage guidelines and color specifications.

  • Vector construction with geometric precision
  • Color variants (full color, monochrome, reversed)
  • Minimum size and clear space guidelines

Validation & Iteration

Designs were presented to peers and instructors for feedback. Multiple rounds of refinement ensured the logos worked across mockup applications including business cards, app icons, and signage.

Results & Learnings

These projects built strong foundations in visual identity design and reinforced the importance of process — starting with research and rationale rather than jumping to aesthetics.

  • Practiced structured logo design process end-to-end
  • Improved precision in vector illustration tools
  • Learned to present and justify design decisions clearly
