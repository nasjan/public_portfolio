Overview

This collection brings together a range of Figma exercises and smaller projects that are not full case studies, but rather focused UI practice pieces. They were created at different stages of learning and experimentation, with the goal of developing skills in visual hierarchy, component thinking, layout structures, and rapid view building.

The emphasis in these works is on UI solutions rather than deep problem definition or user research. They do not represent a complete UX process, but rather individual view-level or interface experiments with limited scope.

Larger, research- and process-driven projects are presented separately in their own case study sections within the portfolio. This collection serves as a complementary look at visual and technical practice in the Figma environment.
