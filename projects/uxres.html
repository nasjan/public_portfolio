S-Kaupat Online Grocery Experience Study

My first independent UX research project and the starting point of my transition into UX/UI design.

Role
UX Researcher
Timeline
Course project
Tools
Google Forms, Miro, ChatGPT, Excel
Team
Solo

Problem

Online grocery services are growing fast, but the user experience still has pain points that need better understanding.

Goal

Study the S-Kaupat online grocery service to find usability issues and improvement opportunities.

My Role

I planned and executed the full research process: survey design, interviews, heuristic evaluation, and analysis.

Outcome

Research report with key findings, user needs, and actionable improvement ideas for the service.

Project Overview

In this project, I studied the user experience of the S-Kaupat online grocery service. I focused mainly on the mobile app, but I also looked at the web version.

The goal was to understand:

  • Who uses the service
  • Why people choose online grocery shopping
  • What problems users face
  • How the service could be improved

This project was part of my studies in Digital Services and UX Design.

My Role

I planned and executed the full research process myself. I was responsible for both research design and analysis.

  • Defined the research questions
  • Designed the survey
  • Conducted user interviews
  • Performed a heuristic evaluation
  • Analyzed results
  • Created improvement ideas

Research Methods

I used three main methods. I also reviewed app store feedback from Google Play.

Survey

31 people answered the online survey. Most users were between 24 and 39 years old. Many used the mobile app weekly or monthly.

Semi Structured Interviews

I interviewed three active users. The interviews helped me understand deeper emotions, habits, and expectations.

Heuristic Evaluation

I evaluated the app using usability principles. I focused on navigation, clarity, feedback, and error handling.

Key Findings

Positive Experiences

  • The service saves time
  • Easy to use in general
  • Clear structure and navigation
  • Bonus system increases loyalty
  • Previous shopping lists are easy to reuse

Challenges

  • Search function can be difficult
  • Payment issues sometimes appear
  • Delivery time windows are wide
  • Product information is sometimes missing
  • Mobile app has more issues than web version

Key insight: Many users use the service not only for ordering, but also for checking product availability, comparing prices, and creating shopping lists.

User Needs

Based on the research, users want:

  • Faster same day delivery
  • Shared shopping lists
  • More flexible delivery pricing
  • Better search results
  • More detailed product information

Some users still prefer physical stores because they want to see products in person.

Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of user centered design.

I learned that:

  • Surveys give structure, but interviews reveal real emotions
  • Users do not always behave as they say
  • Small usability issues can strongly affect trust
  • Good communication and transparency are important in digital services

If I continued this project, I would:

  • Test a prototype with improved search
  • Design a clearer pricing structure
  • Improve delivery experience communication
