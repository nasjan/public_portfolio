UX Research

A usability research project applying heuristic evaluation and user testing methods to evaluate and improve an existing digital product.

Problem

An existing product had usability issues that were not backed by structured research or evidence-based evaluation.

Goal

Overview

This project focused on applying structured UX research methods to evaluate a digital product. The work combined expert-based evaluation with empirical user testing to generate actionable design recommendations.

Problem & Context

The evaluated product had known usability friction, but no formal research had been done to identify specific issues or prioritize improvements. The project aimed to fill that gap with structured, evidence-based evaluation.

Research & Insights

I conducted a heuristic evaluation using Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, followed by moderated usability testing with participants to validate and extend the findings.

  • Heuristic evaluation identified issues with visibility of system status and error prevention
  • User testing revealed navigation confusion in multi-step flows
  • Participants consistently struggled with the same three interaction patterns

Key finding: The most critical issues were not about visual design but about unclear system feedback — users didn't know if their actions had been registered.

Ideation & Decisions

Findings were organized by severity and frequency. I mapped each issue to specific heuristics and proposed concrete improvements prioritized by impact and implementation effort.

  • Severity rating for each finding (critical, major, minor)
  • Mapped to specific Nielsen heuristics
  • Prioritized by user impact and fix complexity

Design Solution

The deliverable was a comprehensive research report with annotated screenshots, severity-rated findings, and specific improvement recommendations for each identified issue.

  • Annotated problem areas with screenshots
  • Recommendation cards with before/after suggestions
  • Summary of quick wins vs. longer-term improvements

Validation & Iteration

The research findings were cross-validated between the heuristic evaluation and user testing results. Issues found in both methods were given higher priority in the final recommendations.

Results & Learnings

The project demonstrated how combining expert evaluation with user testing produces more reliable and actionable results than either method alone.

  • Delivered a prioritized list of 12 improvement recommendations
  • Learned the value of triangulating research methods
  • Strengthened skills in moderated usability testing
  • Would add quantitative metrics in future research projects
