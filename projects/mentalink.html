Mentalink

A concept app that helps people find the right therapist based on their preferences and needs.

Role
UX Research & UI Design
Timeline
Course project period
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Google Forms
Team
Solo

Problem

Finding a therapist is hard. People often do not know where to start or what kind of help they need.

Goal

Design a clear matching flow that helps users find the right therapist quickly and with confidence.

My Role

UX research, user flows, wireframes, and high fidelity prototype in Figma.

Outcome

A complete prototype with personas, user flows, wireframes, and tested high fidelity screens.

Overview

Mentalink is a student project where I designed a concept app for matching people with therapists. The focus was on making the search process feel simple and safe. I wanted users to feel comfortable from the first screen.

The project covered the full UX process from research to a working prototype in Figma.

Problem and Context

Many people struggle to find the right therapist. The process feels confusing because there are too many options and not enough guidance. This makes people give up or delay getting help.

This was a course project at Haaga Helia University of Applied Sciences for the UX Design module.

Research and Insights

I started with a survey and looked at competing services. I wanted to understand what makes people feel comfortable choosing a therapist online.

  • Users want a short, guided process instead of browsing long lists
  • Trust signals like photos and qualifications are important
  • Simple language matters because medical terms feel confusing

Key insight: People need to feel in control. They want to set their own preferences and see why a therapist was recommended to them.

Ideation and Decisions

I designed the flow around a short questionnaire that matches users with therapists based on their answers. The interface shows clear profiles with relevant details so users can make an informed choice.

  • Short questionnaire to understand user needs
  • Matching algorithm that shows the most relevant therapists first
  • Clean therapist profiles with photos, specialties, and availability

Design Solution

The final prototype in Figma includes the full flow from onboarding to booking. The design uses calming colors and clear typography to create a safe and professional feeling.

  • Onboarding screens that explain the process
  • Step by step questionnaire with simple language
  • Results page with matched therapist profiles
  • Booking flow with calendar and confirmation

Validation and Iteration

I tested the prototype with classmates and collected feedback on the flow and visual design. The main finding was that the questionnaire felt clear, but some users wanted more details on the results page.

  • Added more information to therapist profile cards
  • Made the booking button more visible
  • Improved spacing on mobile screens

Results and Learnings

The project taught me the full UX design process from research to a testable prototype. I learned how important trust and clarity are when designing for sensitive topics like mental health.

  • Completed the full UX process from research to high fidelity prototype
  • Learned to design for trust and emotional safety
  • Practiced usability testing and iterating based on feedback
  • Would add more user testing with people outside the class in a future version
